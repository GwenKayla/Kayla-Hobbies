It's sooo ME

I am a firm believer that MY Life isn't just about living for the future: Going to School..Become a Doctor..Earn Money..Travel..Die

It sure feels like it sometimes tho with how much of my time is taken up by schoolwork, responsibilities towards my family and the house, and other learning journeys (which i am thankful of)

But I've been discouraged since I was a child to follow my passions, like after-school sports( soccer, arnis, muay thai) and other artistic endeavors, just so I can focus on School&Home, just because my end goal is becoming a doctor and fulfilling my filial duties. The words "Pagfocus lang sa pagtuon, ayaw sigeg entertain anang walay pulos" is a staple in my childhood

Nowadays I have vowed to enjoy the present too and fill my Free time with childhood hobbies that I have dreamed of, collecting each one of them like pokemon, because what is life if not to work hard, contribute to society, BUT ALSO Enjoy💗

I am so looking forward to add on to my Hobbies and Passion Projects
