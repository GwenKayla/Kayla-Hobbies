It's sooo ME

I have always loved reading since I was a kid. It all started with a book my tutor at the time gave to me called "Utterly me, Clarice Bean" by Lauren Child. It's such a cute hardcover book about a girl named Clarice Bean and her daily life. I kept rereading that one book as many times as I could. Then I branched out to other books and started reading "The Life and Works of Jose Rizal" by Gregorio F. Zaide and Sonia M. Zaide. From then on my love for reading grew greater and iI started reading the whole Disney encyclopedias belonging to my Father and Aunts. That passions grew over the years but sometimes due to life circumstances I would be in a reading slump, but I always find myself back to myself and reading time and time again.

My favourite genres recently are Sci-fi, Fantasy, Horror, Thriller, and Romance. I read physical books, E-books, and even Fanfics on AO3
